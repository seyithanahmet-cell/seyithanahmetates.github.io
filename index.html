About me

I am a Professor of Economics at the Social Sciences University of Ankara, Türkiye. My work brings together economic policy, innovation, entrepreneurship, development and the ways technology reshapes societies and economies.

My academic journey has included research appointments at Harvard University, National Taiwan University and the University of Oxford. I have also contributed to international projects and advisory work involving organizations such as UNIDO and the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development.

Alongside my academic work, I serve as Chair of the Supervisory Board of the Turkish Green Crescent Society (Yeşilay).
